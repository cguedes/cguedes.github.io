Hi, I'm Carlos Guedes — a dad, husband, and software engineer living on the west coast of Europe, in sunny Portugal. I’ve been working in software development and teaching for over 20 years, with a strong focus on full-stack development and web technologies.

Throughout my career, I’ve had the chance to work on all sorts of projects — from small websites to large-scale platforms — using a mix of technologies and frameworks. I enjoy building clean, reliable software and figuring out how to make complex ideas easier to understand and use. Teaching for over a decade also gave me the opportunity to sharpen my communication skills and learn how to work closely with people from different backgrounds and roles.

I’ve led development teams, helped set technical direction for products, and worked directly with leadership teams to align technology with business goals. One of the highlights was leading a team of developers, for a New York company, to implement a microservices architecture for a startup, applying agile methodologies to keep things moving smoothly and adapt as the project evolved.

These days, I’m still excited about building things that solve real problems, learning new tools and approaches, and sharing what I’ve learned along the way. I believe in keeping things simple, working collaboratively, and writing code that’s easy to read and maintain.

When I’m not coding, you’ll probably find me spending time with my family, on a Padel court, watching a tv show, playing a video game or on a boardgame match.
